# Direct attack on the smoothed Liouville estimate

The working paper is now **Signed Liouville sums and local energy estimates**, version 2.2, 7 November 2026.

**RH has not been proved.** This revision retains the complete preceding manuscript and adds proofs, footnotes, a localization diagram and two appendices. The new arguments do not depend on an unrefereed zero-free theorem. Optional deductions in the retained literature section still identify their external assumptions.

The desired estimate remains

\[
B(X)=\sum_{n\ge1}\lambda(n)e^{-n/X}
       =O_\varepsilon(X^{1/2+\varepsilon}).
\]

## Proved analytic construction

For every bounded sequence, not just Liouville, polynomial reconstruction with
\(m=\lceil8\log_2(2X)\rceil\) gives

\[
|B(X)|^2\le10(m+2)
 \int_{X/2}^{2X}|B(Y)|^2\frac{dY}{Y}+X^{-4}.
\]

The proof is explicit: Taylor approximation on a complex disk, followed by a Legendre evaluation kernel on a real interval. It loses a logarithm, rather than a power from using only a derivative bound.

The energy diagonal has the rigorous evaluation

\[
0<D(X)-\left(\frac{3X}{4}-\log2\right)<\frac1{4X}.
\]

The signed off-diagonal upper bound at scale \(X^{1+\varepsilon}\) remains unproved.

## Proved arithmetic comparison

Let \(f_y(p)=-1\) for primes \(p\le y\), and \(f_y(p)=1\) above \(y\). The exact finite-product estimate is

\[
\left|\sum_n f_y(n)e^{-n/X}-c_yX\right|
\le X^\sigma\prod_{p\le y}
 \frac{1+p^{-\sigma}}{1-p^{-\sigma}},
\qquad c_y=\prod_{p\le y}\frac{p-1}{p+1}.
\]

Elementary Chebyshev bounds alone show that at \(y=(\log X)^2\) this error is \(O(X^{1/2+o(1)})\), whereas \(c_yX\ge X/(\log X)^4\). Thus small-prime agreement and complete multiplicativity do not give a uniform square-root estimate.

Writing \(\lambda=f_y*h_y\), with \(h_y(d)=2^{\omega(d)}\lambda(d)\) on \(y\)-rough integers, isolates the exact large-prime task:

\[
\sum_{\substack{d>1\\P^-(d)>y}}
h_y(d)\sum_n f_y(n)e^{-nd/X}
=-c_yX+O_\varepsilon(X^{1/2+\varepsilon}).
\]

This task is not proved. The tail does exhibit a double zero at \(s=1\) in an absolutely convergent Abel weight. Transferring that cancellation to the displayed exponential kernel would require a signed discrepancy estimate that has not been established.

## Proved pole constraints

A self-contained nonnegative-Mellin-transform argument strengthens the pole test to both signs. If a zero \(\rho\), of order \(r\), has \(\operatorname{Re}\rho=\beta>1/2\), then

\[
\limsup\frac{B(X)}{X^\beta(\log X)^{r-1}}\ge C_\rho,\qquad
\liminf\frac{B(X)}{X^\beta(\log X)^{r-1}}\le-C_\rho,
\quad
C_\rho=\frac{r|\Gamma(\rho)\zeta(2\rho)|}{|\zeta^{(r)}(\rho)|}.
\]

The real pole also gives the unconditional lower constraint

\[
\limsup_{X\to\infty}\frac1X
\int_{X/2}^{2X}|B(Y)|^2\frac{dY}{Y}
\ge\frac{3\pi}{8\zeta(1/2)^2}.
\]

An eventual inequality \(B(X)\le0\) would imply RH **and simplicity** of all nontrivial zeros. No eventual-sign theorem is proved. The negative real residue alone cannot exclude positive excursions from an off-line zero.

The original exact checks and the new checks/attack_exact.py pass. New finite checks cover the Legendre reproducing identities, diagonal-error coefficients, prime convolutions, coprime pairs and rational sign certificates. They do not verify RH or the analytic asymptotics. The analytic arguments in this revision have not been formalized in Lean.

No claim is made that these classical-method constructions are new to the literature. The objective of an unconditional RH proof has not been reached.

## Audit of the missing bridge

The added appendix constructs, for every fixed k and any prescribed beta between one-half and one, bounded real coefficients with the first k logarithmic moments at one exactly zero. Their exponential sums nevertheless have two-sided growth of order X^beta. The proof solves a finite Vandermonde system, retains a pair of shifted-zeta poles and estimates the sum by Euler's gamma integral with a bounded error. The construction is not multiplicative and is not a counterexample to RH. It proves that the local Abel cancellations alone do not justify the missing global estimate.
