\section{Exact normalization and rounding controls}\label{round26:app:rounding}
The finite-window normalization in the new construction agrees with the signed energy of Section~\ref{sec:fejer}. The verification script \texttt{anc/check\_exact.py} tests the prefix and suffix identity for all $2046$ sign strings of lengths one through ten. The infinite construction is controlled by the exact discrepancy identity
\[
 \sum_{y_0<p\le t}(I_p-q_p)=\lfloor S(t)\rfloor-S(t)\in(-1,0].
\]
Summation by parts, rather than a finite search, gives the holomorphic centered prime series. The sharp leading-coefficient statement uses the Euler-product inequality for every prime where a sign is changed. Finite prime agreement alone does not impose that coefficient.
