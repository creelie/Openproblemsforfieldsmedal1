# Zeta Zeros and the Exact Growth of Signed Liouville Energies

Complete integrated research revision v2.5.0, 64 pages.

The complete manuscript incorporates a fixed deterministic completely multiplicative construction with all logarithmic savings and maximal Abel vanishing but an energy exponent 2 max(Theta,beta), plus rigidity of the exact Abel coefficient. RH and GRH remain open.

The complete earlier manuscript, its active TeX inputs and figures are retained. The additions include diagrams, footnotes, detailed proofs, primary references and an appendix of exact controls. The new arguments have written proofs and finite checks. No new Lean formalization or journal-acceptance claim is made.
